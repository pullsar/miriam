# Funeral Mass Readings & Funeral Rites for Prof. Miriam Ngozi Mgbakor

> **Final working master — English / Igbo**
>
> Selected readings: **Wisdom 3:1–9 · Psalm 23 · 2 Timothy 4:6–8 · John 14:1–6**
>
> This document consolidates the Catholic funeral-booklet pages supplied for this project, formal Igbo Catholic lectionary witnesses, and the Catholic Igbo Missal app research. The Igbo Scripture is presented as a **harmonized Catholic funeral reading in normalized modern Igbo orthography**. It is intended for proclamation and programme preparation; it is not presented as a photographic/facsimile transcription of one particular printed edition.
>
> **Ordinary Eucharistic Prayer and Communion Rite texts are intentionally omitted.**

# 1. Reception of the Body Before Mass
## Nnabata Ahụ Onye Nwụrụ Anwụ Tupu Ebido Misa

*The priest sprinkles the body/coffin with holy water.*

### English

**V. The Lord be with you.**  
**R. And with your spirit.**

I bless the body of our sister **Miriam** with the holy water that recalls her Baptism, of which Saint Paul writes:

> All of us who were baptised into Christ Jesus were baptised into his death. By Baptism into his death we were buried together with him, so that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might live a new life. For if we have been united with him in a death like his, we shall also be united with him in a resurrection like his.

### Igbo

**V. Onyenweanyị nọnyere unu.**  
**Az. Nọnyekwaara mmụọ gị.**

Eji m mmiri nsọ agọzi ahụ nwanne anyị bụ **Miriam**. Mmiri nsọ a na-echetara anyị Baptizim ya, nke Pọl dị asọ dere banyere ya sị:

> Anyị niile mere Baptizim n’ime Jesu Kristi, e mere anyị Baptizim n’ọnwụ ya. Ya bụ, site na Baptizim n’ọnwụ ya, e liri anyị na ya, ka dịka Kristi si n’ike na ebube nke Nna bilie n’ọnwụ, otu ahụkwa ka anyị ga-esi na-ebi ndụ ọhụrụ. Ọ bụrụ na anyị na Kristi ejikọọla n’oyiyi ọnwụ ya, anyị na ya ga-ejikọkwa n’oyiyi mbilite n’ọnwụ ya.

### Let Us Pray — Ka Anyị Rịọ Arịrịọ

#### English

Lord, we humbly ask your mercy. Accept the soul of your servant **Miriam**. Forgive whatever sins she may have committed through human frailty. Free her from the bonds of death and admit her into everlasting life.

**Through Christ our Lord. Amen.**

#### Igbo

Onyenweanyị, anyị ji umeala arịọ ebere Gị. Were obiọma nara mkpụrụ obi nwaodibo Gị **Miriam**. Ọ bụrụ na o mehiere site n’adịghị ike nke mmadụ, biko gbaghara ya. Tọpụ ya n’agbụ niile nke ọnwụ, ka o gafee na ndụ ebeebe.

**Site na Kristi Dinwenụ anyị. Amen.**

# 3. Opening Prayer
## Ekpere Mbido

### English

God of mercy, you are the hope of sinners and the joy of saints. We pray for our sister **Miriam**, whose body we honour with Christian burial. Give her happiness with your saints and raise up her body in glory on the last day, to be in your presence forever.

Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you and the Holy Spirit, God, forever and ever.

**Amen.**

### Igbo

Chineke Onye ebere, Gị bụ nchekwube ndị mmehie na aṅụrị ndị nsọ. Anyị na-arịọ maka nwanne anyị bụ **Miriam**, onye anyị na-eli ahụ ya n’usoro ndị otu Kristi. Nye ya aṅụrị n’ebe ndị nsọ Gị nọ, ma kulitekwa ahụ ya n’ebube n’ụbọchị ikpeazụ, ebe ọ ga-anọ n’ihu Gị ebeebe.

Site na Dinwenụ anyị Jesu Kristi, Nwa Gị, onye Gị na Ya na Mmụọ Nsọ dị ndụ na-achị, Chukwu, ebeebe ebeebe.

**Amen.**

# 4. First Reading — Wisdom 3:1–9
## Ihe Ọgụgụ Nke Mbụ — Amamihe 3:1–9

### English

**A reading from the Book of Wisdom.**

*God accepted them as a sacrificial burnt offering.*

The souls of the virtuous are in the hands of God; no torment shall ever touch them. In the eyes of the unwise, they appeared to die; their passing looked like a disaster, their leaving us like annihilation; but they are in peace.

If they experienced suffering as people see it, their hope was rich with immortality. Their affliction was slight, but great will their blessings be. God put them to the test and proved them worthy to be with him. He tested them like gold in a furnace and accepted them as a sacrificial burnt offering.

When the time of his visitation comes, they will shine out and run like sparks through dry stubble. They shall judge nations and rule over peoples, and the Lord will be their King forever.

Those who trust in him will understand the truth; those who remain faithful will live with him in love, for grace and mercy await those he has chosen.

**The Word of the Lord.**  
**R. Thanks be to God.**

### Igbo

**Ihe ọgụgụ e wetara n’Akwụkwọ Amamihe.**

*Chineke nara ha dịka aja nsure ọkụ.*

Mkpụrụ obi ndị eziomume dị n’aka Chineke; o nweghị ahụhụ ọbụla ga-emetụ ha. N’anya ndị nzuzu, ha dị ka ndị nwụrụ anwụ; ngafe ha dị ka ọdachi, nhapụ ha hapụrụ anyị dị ka ịla n’iyi; ma ha nọ n’udo.

Ọ bụrụ na ha tara ahụhụ dịka ndị mmadụ si hụ ya, nchekwube ha jupụtara n’anwụghị anwụ. Ahụhụ ha dị ntakịrị, ma ngọzi ha ga-adị ukwuu. Chineke n’onwe ya anwaleela ha, wee chọpụta na ha tosiri ibinyere ya. Ọ nụchaala ha dịka a na-anụcha ọlaedo n’ọkụ, wee nara ha dịka aja nsure ọkụ.

Mgbe oge nleta ya bịara, ha ga-achawapụta dịka ìhè; ha ga-agba dịka ikerike ọkụ n’ahịhịa kpọrọ nkụ. Ha ga-achị mba niile na ndị mmadụ niile; **Dinwenụ ga-abụ Eze ha ebeebe.**

Ndị tụkwasịrị obi na ya ga-aghọta eziokwu; ndị kwụdosiri ike n’okwukwe ga-ebinyere ya n’ịhụnanya, n’ihi na amara na ebere na-echere ndị ọ họpụtara.

**Okwu nke Osebụrụwa.**  
**Az. Ekele dịrị Chukwu.**

# 5. Responsorial Psalm — Psalm 23
## Abụ Ọma na Azịza — Abụ Ọma 23

### English

**Response: The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

The Lord is my shepherd; I lack nothing.  
He gives me rest in green pastures  
and leads me beside peaceful waters.  
He renews my strength  
and guides me along the right path  
for the honour of his name.

**R. The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

Even though I walk through a valley dark with death,  
I will fear no evil, for you are with me.  
Your rod and your staff are beside me,  
and they give me courage.

**R. The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

You prepare a table for me  
in the sight of those who oppose me.  
You anoint my head with oil;  
my cup is filled to overflowing.

**R. The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

Surely goodness and mercy will follow me  
all the days of my life,  
and I shall dwell in the house of the Lord  
for length of days.

**R. The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

### Igbo

**Azịza: Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m;  
o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.  
Ọ na-edina m n’ala ahịhịa ndụ;  
ọ na-eduga m n’akụkụ mmiri dị jụụ.  
Ọ na-enye mkpụrụ obi m ume ọhụrụ;  
ọ na-edu m n’ụzọ eziomume  
n’ihi aha ya.

**Az. Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

Ọ bụladị na m na-aga  
na ndagwurugwu nke ọnwụ,  
egwu ọbụla agaghị atụ m,  
n’ihi na Ị nọnyere m.  
Mkpara Gị na ngwọ Gị  
na-akasi m obi.

**Az. Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

Ị dobeere m nri  
n’ihu ndị iro m.  
Ị tere isi m mmanụ;  
iko m jupụtara nke ukwuu.

**Az. Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

N’ezie, obiọma na ebere ga-eso m  
ụbọchị niile nke ndụ m;  
aga m ebikwa n’ụlọ Dinwenụ  
ruo mgbe ebighị ebi.

**Az. Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

# 6. Second Reading — 2 Timothy 4:6–8
## Ihe Ọgụgụ Nke Abụọ — 2 Timoti 4:6–8

### English

**A reading from the Second Letter of Saint Paul to Timothy.**

Beloved: my life is already being offered, and the time for my departure has arrived. I have fought the good fight, completed the race and kept the faith.

From now on the crown of righteousness is laid up for me. On that Day the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give it to me — and not to me alone, but to all those who have awaited his appearing with love.

**The Word of the Lord.**  
**R. Thanks be to God.**

### Igbo

**Ihe ọgụgụ e wetara n’Akwụkwọ Ozi Nke Abụọ Pọl dị asọ degaara Timoti.**

Onye m hụrụ n’anya: adị m njikere ka e jiri m chụọ aja; oge ịla m eruola. **Alụọla m ezigbo ọgụ, agbaruola m ọsọ ahụ ruo n’isi, akwụdosiela m ike n’okwukwe.**

Site ugbu a e doberela m okpueze nke eziomume, nke Onyenweanyị, ezi Onye Ọkaikpe, ga-enye m n’ụbọchị ahụ. Ọ bụghị naanị m ka ọ ga-enye okpueze ahụ; ọ ga-enyekwa ya ndị niile ji ịhụnanya na-atụ anya ọbịbịa ya.

**Okwu nke Osebụrụwa.**  
**Az. Ekele dịrị Chukwu.**

# 7. Gospel — John 14:1–6
## Oziọma — Jọn 14:1–6

### English

**A reading from the Holy Gospel according to John.**

Jesus said to his disciples:

“Do not let your hearts be troubled. Put your trust in God, and trust in me as well. In my Father’s house there is room for many. If that were not so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you.

And when I have gone and prepared a place for you, I will come again and take you to myself, so that you may be where I am. You know the way to where I am going.”

Thomas said to him, “Lord, we do not know where you are going; how can we know the way?”

Jesus answered, “I am the Way, the Truth and the Life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”

**The Gospel of the Lord.**  
**R. Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.**

### Igbo

**Ihe ọgụgụ nke a si n’Oziọma dị nsọ nke Jọn dere.**

N’oge ahụ, Jesu gwara ndị na-eso ụzọ ya sị:

“Obi alọla unu mmiri. Kwerenụ na Chineke; kwerekwanụ na m. N’ụlọ Nna m, e nwere ọtụtụ ebe obibi. Ọ bụrụ na ọ bụghị otu a, agaara m agwa unu na m na-aga ịkwadoro unu ebe obibi?

Ọ bụrụ na m agaa dozichaara unu ebe obibi, aga m alọghachi kpọrọ unu ka unu nọnyere m, ka ebe m nọ bụrụ ebe unu ga-anọ. Unu ma ụzọ ebe m na-aga.”

Tọmọs sịrị ya, “Onyenweanyị, anyị amaghị ebe Ị na-aga; olee otu anyị ga-esi mata ụzọ ahụ?”

Jesu sịrị ya, **“Mụ onwe m bụ Ụzọ, Eziokwu na Ndụ. Ọ dịghị onye ọbụla ga-abịakwute Nna ma ọ bụghị site na m.”**

**Oziọma nke Osebụrụwa.**  
**Az. Otito dịrị Gị, Kristi.**

# 8. Homily & Prayer of the Faithful
## Ozizi & Ekpere Ndị Kwere Ekwe

*A brief homily follows the Gospel.*

### Introduction — English

In the hope of the resurrection, we place our prayers before God, who is rich in mercy.

### Mbido — Igbo

N’olileanya nke mbilite n’ọnwụ, ka anyị debe arịrịọ anyị n’ihu Chineke, Onye juputara n’ebere.

### 1. For Miriam

**English**

For **Miriam**, who was joined to Christ in Baptism and served God with a joyful and generous heart: that she may be welcomed into the light and peace of God’s presence, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka **Miriam**, onye e jikọtara na Kristi site na Baptizim, onye jiri obi aṅụrị na mmesapụ aka jeere Chineke ozi: ka a nabata ya n’ìhè na udo nke ọnụnọ Chineke, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### 2. For the Church

**English**

For the Church, and for the priests, religious and faith communities who prayed and worshipped with Miriam: that their ministry may continue to bring hope to all who mourn, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka Nzukọ Kraịst, ndị ụkọchukwu, ndị e doro ndụ ha nsọ na òtù okwukwe niile ndị ha na Miriam kpekọrọ ekpere ma fee Chineke: ka ozi ha na-eje gaa n’ihu iweta olileanya nye ndị niile na-eru uju, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### 3. For the Family

**English**

For **Prof. Anthony Mgbakor, Mrs. Diayne Nkechinyelu Opata, Mr. Angelo Mgbakor, the grandchildren and the entire Ogbonna and Mgbakor families**: that God may hold them close and strengthen them with enduring love, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka **Prof. Anthony Mgbakor, Mrs. Diayne Nkechinyelu Opata, Mr. Angelo Mgbakor, ụmụ ụmụ ya, na ezinaụlọ Ogbonna na Mgbakor niile**: ka Chineke debe ha nso n’obi Ya ma were ịhụnanya na-adịgide adịgide gbaa ha ume, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### 4. For ESUT

**English**

For Miriam’s students, colleagues and the whole **ESUT community**: that the knowledge, discipline and motherly encouragement she shared may continue to bear fruit in their lives, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka ụmụakwụkwọ Miriam, ndị ya na ha rụkọrọ ọrụ na obodo **ESUT** niile: ka ihe ọmụma, ịdọ aka ná ntị na agbamume dịka nke nne nke o kesara ha nọgide na-amị mkpụrụ n’ime ndụ ha, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### 5. For Her Communities

**English**

For her sisters, in-laws, friends, choristers and all the communities she gathered around her: that gratitude for her open, joyous and practical love may sustain them through grief, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka ụmụnne ya ndị nwanyị, ndị ọgọ ya, ndị enyi ya, ndị otu ukwe na obodo niile ọ kpọkọtara gburugburu ya: ka ekele maka ịhụnanya ya nke ghe oghe, juputara n’aṅụrị ma gosipụta onwe ya n’omume nyere ha aka ịgafe oge uju a, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### 6. For the Faithful Departed and All Gathered

**English**

For all the faithful departed, and for everyone gathered here today: that our faith in Christ’s victory over death may deepen and that we may one day be reunited in the Father’s house, let us pray to the Lord.

**R. Lord, hear our prayer.**

**Igbo**

Maka ndị niile kwere ekwe nwụrụ anwụ, na maka anyị niile gbakọrọ ebe a taa: ka okwukwe anyị na mmeri Kristi meriri ọnwụ sikwuo ike, ka anyị wee nwee ike izukọta ọzọ otu ụbọchị n’ụlọ Nna, ka anyị rịọ Dinwenụ.

**Az. Dinwenụ, nụrụ arịrịọ anyị.**

### Conclusion

**English**

God of life, hear the prayers we offer in faith. Grant Miriam eternal rest, console all who love her, and keep us faithful until we are gathered into your kingdom.

**Through Christ our Lord. Amen.**

**Igbo**

Chineke nke ndụ, nụrụ arịrịọ ndị anyị ji okwukwe na-ebunye Gị. Nye **Miriam** ezumike ebeebe, kasie ndị niile hụrụ ya n’anya obi, ma chekwaa anyị n’okwukwe ruo mgbe a ga-akpọkọta anyị n’alaeze Gị.

**Site na Kristi Dinwenụ anyị. Amen.**

# 10. Preface for the Dead
## Preface Maka Ndị Nwụrụ Anwụ

> *The following funeral preface may be used by the celebrant according to the Missal. The Eucharistic Prayer itself is omitted.*

### Dialogue

**V. The Lord be with you.**  
**R. And with your spirit.**

**V. Lift up your hearts.**  
**R. We lift them up to the Lord.**

**V. Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.**  
**R. It is right and just.**

### English

It is truly right and just, our duty and our salvation, always and everywhere to give you thanks, Lord, holy Father, almighty and eternal God, through Christ our Lord.

For as one alone he accepted death, so that we might all escape from dying; as one man he chose to die, so that in your sight we all might live forever.

And so, in company with the choirs of Angels, we praise you, and with joy we proclaim:

**Holy, Holy, Holy Lord God of hosts…**

### Igbo

Ọ kwesiri ekwesi, bụrụkwa ihe ziri ezi, ọrụ dịịrị anyị na nzọpụta anyị, ka anyị na-enye Gị ekele mgbe niile na ebe niile, Dinwenụ, Nna dị nsọ, Chineke ji ike niile, Onye dị ebeebe, site na Kristi Dinwenụ anyị.

N’ihi na Ya onwe Ya bụ otu onye ahụ nabatara ọnwụ, ka anyị niile wee ghara ịnwụ; Ya bụ otu onye họọrọ ịnwụ, ka anyị niile wee dịrị Gị ndụ ebighị ebi.

N’ihi nke a, anyị na-esonyere ndị mmụọ ozi na-eto Gị n’aṅụrị sị:

**Nsọ, Nsọ, Nsọ Osebụrụwa Chineke…**

# 11. Final Commendation
## Ekpere Ntohapụ Ikpeazụ

*The coffin may be sprinkled with holy water and incensed.*

### English — Celebrant

With faith in Jesus Christ, we reverently bring the body of our sister **Miriam** to Christian burial. Let us pray with confidence to God, who gives life to all things, that he will raise this mortal body to the perfection and company of the saints.

May God give her a merciful judgment and forgive all her sins. May Christ, the Good Shepherd, lead her safely home to be at peace with God our Father; and may she be happy forever with all the saints in the presence of the eternal King.

### Igbo — Onye Isi Emume

Anyị ka o kwesịrị dịka ezigbo ndị otu Kristi ili ahụ mmadụ n’ụzọ kwesiri ekwesi. Ya mere, ka anyị jiri umeala rịọ Chineke, Onye na-enye ihe niile ndụ, ka O kulite ahụ nwanne anyị bụ **Miriam**, onye anyị na-eli ugbu a, ka o sonye n’otu ndị nsọ.

Ka O gosi ya ebere n’ikpe nke O ga-ekpe ya ma gbaghara ya mmehie ya niile. Nwanne anyị bụ onye Kristi ji ọnwụ Ya gbapụta. Ka ezigbo Onye Nche Atụrụ duru ya n’udo, mee ka ya na Nna dịrị n’udo, ma kpọbata ya n’ọgbakọ nke ndị nsọ, n’aṅụrị na-enweghị ngwụcha n’ihu Eze nke dị ebeebe.

# 12. Song of Farewell
## Ukwe Laa Mma

### English

**V. Saints of God, come to her aid.**  
**R. Come to meet her, Angels of the Lord.**

**V. Receive her soul and present her to God the Most High.**  
**R. Receive her soul and present her to God the Most High.**

**V. May Christ, who called her, take her to himself; may Angels lead her to Abraham’s bosom.**  
**R. Receive her soul and present her to God the Most High.**

**V. Give her eternal rest, O Lord, and may your perpetual light shine upon her.**  
**R. Receive her soul and present her to God the Most High.**

### Igbo

**V. Ndị nsọ nke Chukwu, nyerenụ ya aka.**  
**Az. Ndị mmụọ ozi nke Chineke, zutenụ ya.**

**V. Naranụ mkpụrụ obi ya, duru ya gaa n’ihu Onye Kasị Elu.**

# 20. Textual Basis / Reliability Notes

This master deliberately combines the strongest evidence rather than treating any single internet Igbo Bible as authoritative.

## Scripture readings

### Wisdom 3:1–9

The wording was checked against:

- the photographed bilingual Catholic funeral booklet supplied for this project;
- the Catholic funeral reading tradition used in Nigerian Igbo programmes;
- the structured **PWorld Catholic Missal-English & Igbo** burial object for *Amamihe 3:1–9*;
- formal Nigerian Igbo Catholic lectionary witnesses.

Where the sources differed slightly, the final copy uses modern Igbo spelling and the more suitable liturgical form **Dinwenụ** rather than pronouncing the Divine Name as *Yahweh*.

### Psalm 23

Two Catholic variants were found:

- **Dinwenụ bụ onye nche m; o nwekwaghị ihe m ga-achọ.**
- **Chineke bụ onye nche m; o nweghị ihe m ga-achọ.**

The first was selected because it is strongly attested in formal Igbo Catholic responsorial use and corresponds directly to the requested English response:

> **The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.**

### 2 Timothy 4:6–8

The distinctive Catholic wording was independently confirmed by:

- Nigerian Igbo Catholic lectionary material;
- the PWorld structured burial reading object.

Its identifying sequence is retained:

> **Alụọla m ezigbo ọgụ, agbaruola m ọsọ ahụ ruo n’isi, akwụdosiela m ike n’okwukwe.**

Only verses **6–8** are retained for Miriam’s selected reading.

### John 14:1–6

The reading was checked against:

- formal Catholic Igbo lectionary material;
- Catholic funeral programmes;
- the PWorld structured burial Gospel object;
- an independently attested *Baịbụl Nsọ: Nhazi Katọlik* fingerprint for John 14:6.

The key Catholic ending is retained:

> **Mụ onwe m bụ Ụzọ, Eziokwu na Ndụ.**

## Funeral rites

The funeral-specific rites are adapted primarily from the photographed bilingual Catholic funeral booklet supplied during this project. Personal references have been changed from the deceased in that booklet to **Prof. Miriam Ngozi Mgbakor**, and obvious spelling/OCR defects have been normalized.

## Exclusion

The ordinary **Eucharistic Prayer and Communion Rite** are not reproduced here. The celebrant should use the approved Roman Missal / local diocesan liturgical books for those sections.
