# Prof. Miriam Ngozi Mgbakor Memorial Brochure - Design Specification

## Purpose

Create a professional, beautiful and deeply personal A4 portrait memorial booklet for Prof. Miriam Ngozi Mgbakor. The booklet will celebrate her as a scholar, mentor, woman of faith, chorister, wife, mother, sister and friend. It will draw from the memorial website, the complete live tribute collection, uploaded site photographs, the local family archive and verified public academic sources.

The result should feel like a carefully edited commemorative book, not a generic funeral template.

## Editorial Strategy

The booklet will not reproduce every submission at full length. The live collection contains more than 100 valid tributes and approximately 27,000 words, which cannot fit comfortably within 48 pages alongside a biography, service information and photographs.

Editorial treatment:

1. Print major institutional and representative group tributes first.
2. Give immediate family and the Ogbonna and Mgbakor families strong prominence.
3. Preserve a representative range of voices from church groups, students, colleagues and friends.
4. Use full tributes where length and significance permit; otherwise use faithful excerpts with the contributor clearly credited.
5. Remove exact duplicates and obvious test submissions.
6. Correct clear typographical errors conservatively while preserving each contributor's meaning and voice.
7. Do not invent facts, quotations or relationships.
8. Include a QR code and website address for the complete online tribute collection.

## Narrative Structure

The editorial order moves from public legacy to faith, family and personal memory:

1. **Opening and identity** - cover, title, scripture, immediate family and contents.
2. **Her life and legacy** - brief biography, education, career, consultancy, research, professional memberships and service.
3. **Scholar and mentor** - ESUT Vice-Chancellor; Faculty; Department; postgraduate students; professional and academic groups.
4. **Faith and song** - St. Mulumba Main Choir; ESUT Catholic Community and NFCS; R.I.T.A.; parish communities; clergy and Marian devotion.
5. **At the family hearth** - husband and children; Ogbonna family; Mgbakor family; siblings, in-laws, nieces, nephews and close families.
6. **Friendship across the years** - school friends, colleagues, neighbours, international friends and families such as the Ekweremadu family.
7. **Her joy in pictures** - a paced photographic sequence showing academic life, worship, celebrations, travel, family and ordinary warmth.
8. **Farewell and acknowledgement** - selected closing prayers, programme information, acknowledgements, QR code and back cover.

## Art Direction

### Visual Character

Refined editorial memorial book: dignified, warm, personal, Catholic in spirit and contemporary without losing ceremony.

Use:

- Asymmetric editorial grids
- Generous warm-paper negative space
- Large, carefully cropped photographs
- Restrained antique-gold rules and labels
- Pull quotations drawn from actual tributes
- Occasional drop caps and two-column reading pages
- Section openers with clear pacing

Avoid:

- Generic roses, clouds, doves and template ornament
- Decorative scripts for names or body copy
- Excessive gold, gradients or shadows
- Rounded app-style cards
- Dense text walls without hierarchy
- Arbitrary photo collages with no narrative purpose

## Typography System

### Display: Libre Bodoni

Use for the cover name, chapter titles and large pull quotations. Its high-contrast forms create an elegant, commemorative editorial tone. Avoid very small sizes and long paragraphs.

### Reading: Source Serif 4

Use for biography and tribute text. It is designed for extended reading and supports a comfortable, literary voice.

### Factual and Metadata: Public Sans

Use for dates, labels, bylines, contributor roles, captions, folios and contents entries. It provides clean contrast to the serif families.

### Print Type Scale

- Cover name: approximately 42-52 pt depending on crop and line breaks
- Chapter title: 28-34 pt
- Section heading: 18-22 pt
- Pull quote: 15-19 pt
- Body: 10.5-11 pt, never below 10 pt
- Body leading: 14.5-16 pt
- Contributor/byline: 8.5-9.5 pt
- Caption/folio: 8-9 pt, never below 8 pt

Body line length should normally remain between 45 and 72 characters. Long tribute pages may use two columns with a generous gutter.

Small text must meet at least 4.5:1 contrast; large display text must meet at least 3:1. Gold is primarily an accent and should not replace body text.

## Text Over Photographs

Readability over imagery is a non-negotiable production requirement.

1. Prefer placing text in a separate solid colour field rather than directly over a photograph.
2. If text must overlay an image, use a deliberately chosen quiet image region plus a controlled scrim or gradient.
3. The scrim must be strong enough that the measured effective text contrast meets the approved threshold across the entire text area.
4. Do not place small type over faces, patterned clothing, bright windows, foliage, event lighting or mixed highlights.
5. Use warm ivory or white over darkened images and ink navy over deliberately lightened images.
6. Add a subtle solid backing panel if a crop cannot provide uniform contrast.
7. Inspect every image-text page at actual print size and in grayscale; colour alone must not create readability.
8. Never use outlined text, heavy drop shadows or glow effects to rescue a poor image crop.

## Quality Assurance

Before delivery:

- Confirm every page renders and the final page count is a multiple of four
- Inspect every page at 100% zoom
- Check bleed, trim, safe area, image resolution and spine/gutter proximity
- Verify no clipped text, widows, orphans, overflow or awkward column breaks
- Verify all text/photo overlays and colour pairs for contrast
- Review the entire booklet in grayscale
- Check contributor names and roles against the source submissions
- Check dates, family names and institutional titles consistently
- Confirm quotations are faithful and clearly attributed
- Confirm the QR code scans from a printed-size proof
- Reopen the final PDF and verify fonts are embedded and pages render correctly
