Her story

Her life

A life remembered in full colour

“Miriam’s love was open, joyous and practical.”

Across family, classrooms, church communities and friendships, the same qualities reverberate in Miriam’s life: disciplined work, an open manner, a motherly welcome, a love of music and an infectious joy that made others happy.

Born on 21 November 1960, Miriam became a scholar and Professor of Agricultural Economics, yet the names spoken with most affection were the familiar ones: Mimi, May May, Aunty Mimi, Mummy and Sister Mgbakor. To her husband, Professor Anthony Mgbakor; her daughter, Mrs. Diayne Nkechinyelu Opata; her son, Mr. Angelo Mgbakor; her grandchildren and the wider Mgbakor and Ogbonna families, she was the bright and dependable centre of home.

She earned a B.Sc. in Botany from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka in 1984, then pursued postgraduate study in Agricultural Economics, Farm Management and Finance at Enugu State University of Science and Technology. Her appetite for learning took her to Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire, for a diploma in French in 2005, and to Haifa, Israel, where she completed the MASHAV training programme in microcredit and support services for women in small businesses in 2006. She earned her doctorate in Agricultural Economics in 2016.

Miriam joined ESUT as Lecturer II on 31 January 2008 and rose, through years of teaching, research, service and mentorship, to become a Professor on 1 October 2023. She served as Head of the Department of Agricultural Economics and Extension and gave herself generously to the students whose futures mattered deeply to her.

Her Catholic faith found joyful expression in community and in song. As a chorister, colleague, friend and family woman, Miriam celebrated the happiness of others as though it were her own. The photographs and tributes gathered here preserve something of that remarkable warmth.
